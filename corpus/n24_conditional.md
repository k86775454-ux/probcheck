section: 2.4 Conditional probability
updated: 2026-09-20

The conditional probability of A given B is P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B). It is defined only when P(B) > 0.

If A and B are independent and P(B) > 0, then P(A|B) = P(A): knowing that B happened does not change the probability of A.
