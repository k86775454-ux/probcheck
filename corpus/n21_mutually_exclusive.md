section: 2.1 Mutually exclusive events
updated: 2026-09-20
---
Two events A and B are mutually exclusive (disjoint) if they cannot both occur. Then A∩B is empty, so P(A∩B) = 0.

For mutually exclusive events the addition rule simplifies to P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B).
