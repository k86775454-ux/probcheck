## Screening criteria (written before screening)

1. Examines human interaction with AI chatbots or conversational assistants, preferably in learning contexts.
2. Investigates uncertainty communication, explanations, or transparency in AI responses.
3. Reports findings about users' trust, reliance, verification behaviour, error detection, or learning outcomes.
