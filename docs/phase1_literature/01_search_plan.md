# Search plan

Problem sentence:  
Students trying to study STEM topics struggle to decide whether to trust answers from an AI study assistant because the interface does not clearly communicate uncertainty or provide verifiable evidence.
## Concept blocks (two or three terms each; take synonyms from the first abstracts you read)

| Block | What it covers | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| A | Users deciding whether to trust or verify AI answers | trust; reliance; verification |
| B | How the system communicates uncertainty and supporting evidence | uncertainty; transparency; explanation |
| C | AI assistants used for studying | chatbot; conversational AI; learning assistant |
## One natural-language question (for tools that read questions)
How does communicating uncertainty affect students' trust in and reliance on answers from AI study assistants?
## One Boolean query (for tools that match strings: OR inside a block, AND between blocks)
(trust OR reliance OR verification) AND (uncertainty OR transparency OR explanation) AND (chatbot OR "conversational AI" OR "learning assistant")
