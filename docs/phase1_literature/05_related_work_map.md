# Related-work map and gap sentence

## Problem sentence

Students trying to study STEM topics struggle to decide whether to trust answers from an AI study assistant because the interface does not clearly communicate uncertainty or provide verifiable evidence.

## Themes (two or three; name each in four words)

### Theme A — Uncertainty cues and reliance
- **P1 — Li et al. (2026):** In STEM learning, constant verbal uncertainty reduced adoption of incorrect chatbot responses, with mixed learning effects; shorter answers helped detect logical fallacies without increasing alternative-resource use.
- **P2 — Kim et al. (2024):** In medical question answering, first-person uncertainty reduced agreement with the system and improved accuracy; exploratory analysis associated this with reduced, rather than eliminated, overreliance.
- **P4 — Spatharioti et al. (2025):** LLM-based search offered speed benefits but users overrelied on incorrect outputs; color-coded highlighting helped detect errors and improve decision accuracy.

### Theme B — Verification through explanation design
- **P5 — Fok and Weld (2024):** A theoretical synthesis argues that explanations can support complementary decision performance when they enable users to verify AI advice.

### Theme C — Deliberation effort and acceptance
- **P3 — Buçinca et al. (2021):** Cognitive forcing reduced overreliance relative to simple explanation designs, but the most effective designs received less favourable subjective ratings; benefits also varied with cognitive motivation.

## Gap sentence

These five sources establish that uncertainty cues can affect reliance and accuracy (A), that explanations can support answer verification when they enable users to verify AI advice (B), and that encouraging deliberation can involve acceptance and effort trade-offs (C), but none of these five sources directly evaluates whether adding a one-click link to a checked source passage to the same verbal uncertainty cue improves students' detection of incorrect STEM study answers; that is the bounded gap this project addresses.
